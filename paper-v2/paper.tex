\begin{abstract}
In \mbox{Gemma-2-9B-it}, we locate and edit two weight-level components of the
decision to help a person at the expense of an assigned task, and show they
play distinct functional roles: \emph{writers} carry cost-independent
prosocial value, while \emph{suppressors} implement cost-gated inhibition of
welfare-motivated action. On a graded cost axis with a clause-matched
non-welfare control, suppressor-removal effects grow from $+0.06$ (helping
free) to $+0.49$--$0.53$ (helping costly; welfare$\times$cost interaction CI
$[+0.026,+0.169]$), while remaining flat with cost on the non-welfare axis;
writer removal costs $\approx-0.20$ at every price point,
welfare-preferentially (interaction CI $[-0.117,-0.070]$). The edits are
rank-1 orthogonalizations of components selected by direct feature
attribution from a \emph{moderately distributed} push--pull pattern. On
held-out scenario families, writer removal reduces forced-choice helping by
$-0.184$ (CI $[-0.236,-0.133]$; $\ge4.3\times$ selective over four control
cells; $3.2\times$ the observed random-component leak; $z=-10$ vs.\
norm-matched random directions on the same weights); suppressor removal
increases it ($+0.150$) but also moves task persistence — the factorial assay
above separates its cost gate from that flat engagement component. In the one
Empathy-in-Action game scenario with behavioral headroom, suppressor — but not
writer — edits transfer to interactive action allocation: across 13 pooled
seeds, $0/13$ task completions vs.\ $2/13$ baseline and $+4.92$ supportive
verbal actions (CI $[+1.92,+7.85]$, sign-test $p=.006$; $9/13$ runs at the
interaction ceiling), without detectable degradation on a 400-question MMLU
sample or WikiText perplexity. The underlying direction — derived from
matched-lexicon minimal pairs at block 20/42 and passing a diagnostic
surface-form robustness test (AUROC 0.911 vs.\ 0.827 matched reference, 13
calibrated sources) — carries $\sim$88\% of baseline choice separation under
full ablation on this assay. The same dependence does not replicate unchanged
in \mbox{Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct}: an M-derived direction is perfectly decodable
on held-out families but fails the warmth/persona specificity profile, full
ablation erases only $\sim$5.5\% of choice separation (linear fractional dose
curves, null random-direction controls, task control quiet), selected writer
edits produce a smaller, format-sensitive effect ($\approx$18--20\% of the
full-direction effect) and suppressor edits are null. We report the
chronological audit trail and pre-registered confirmatory amendments,
including 3--4$\times$ in-sample inflation observed directly in the Llama
replication — and its absence ($\sim$14\% shrinkage) for Gemma's writer edit.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A functional decomposition of a costly-helping mechanism:
        cost-independent prosocial value (writers) vs.\ cost-gated inhibition
        (suppressors), established by a graded cost axis with a clause-matched
        non-welfare control (E18).
  \item Held-out weight-edit effects with their differing selectivity
        profiles: a writer effect $3.2\times$ larger than the observed
        random-component leak, passing a four-cell selectivity criterion;
        a suppressor effect that transfers to behavior but carries a
        non-selective engagement footprint (E13, E14b, E14c, E15).
  \item One-sided interactive transfer: suppressor (not writer) edits change
        action allocation in the single EIA scenario with behavioral headroom;
        the self-assessment measure is invariant (zero variance), giving an
        intention--action dissociation of limited evidential weight
        (E16, E16b).
  \item A purified direction with a controlled transfer profile and
        diagnostic-grade surface-form robustness on 13 calibrated sources
        (E08, E19b).
  \item An assay-bounded cross-model dependence contrast (full-direction
        ablation erases $\sim$88\% of choice separation in Gemma vs.\
        $\sim$5.5\% in Llama; linear fractional dose curves, random-direction
        controls null), with a partial, format-sensitive Llama writer effect
        and a null suppressor replication (E17, E17b, E18b).
  \item A chronological audit trail with pre-registered confirmatory
        amendments: in-sample inflation observed directly in the Llama
        replication (3--4$\times$) and absent for Gemma's writer edit
        ($\sim$14\% shrinkage); a stimulus-artifact robustness cell caught and
        regenerated with a strict decision-audit pipeline (E19, E19b).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Component specificity is bounded, not absolute.} Removing the
        direction from six arbitrary components moves the primary outcome
        $-0.057$; the writer effect is $3.2\times$ larger. The E18 random
        interaction is likewise nonzero ($+0.018$), $\sim$5$\times$ smaller
        than targeted interactions.
  \item \textbf{Game evidence is one-sided and scenario-local.} The
        pre-registered composite across five scenarios was null (three lacked
        dynamic range; the official judge rubric scored every listener run
        2/2 — the reported signal is below rubric resolution). Writers were
        null in games; $9/13$ suppressor runs hit the say-count ceiling;
        semantic repetitiveness and miscalibrated over-support are not ruled
        out (``supportive verbal action,'' not ``better help'').
  \item \textbf{Mechanism claims concern one model.} The Llama replication
        yields a partial, format-sensitive writer effect and a null suppressor
        effect; two models differing in architecture and training cannot
        localize the cause of the difference.
  \item \textbf{Robustness is diagnostic-grade.} 13 source pairs, one
        perturbation set, one model/block; not an equivalence claim. A strict
        immediate-action audit retained only 13/34 original cell-A sources,
        weakening unfiltered cell-A transfer numbers.
  \item \textbf{Capability scope.} Only a 400-question MMLU sample and
        WikiText perplexity were tested; the writer-edit interval rules out
        losses larger than $\sim$1.25pp on that sample.
  \item \textbf{Dependence is not mediation.} Gemma's full-direction ablation
        also moves task persistence ($+0.58$); the 88\% figure is an
        assay-bounded dependence estimate, not strict mediation or a fully
        selective intervention.
\end{itemize}
