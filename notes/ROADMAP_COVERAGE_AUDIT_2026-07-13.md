# Roadmap Coverage Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Scope:** map every scientific program in `ROADMAP.md` to a frozen
preregistration, an explicit historical/exploratory ceiling, or a remaining
design gap. This audit does not modify the living roadmap. The authoritative
execution and data-use rules remain `SCIENTIFIC_PREREG_INDEX_2026-07-13.md`
and `EXECUTION_ORDER_2026-07-13.md`.

## Result

All currently executable experiments capable of supporting a new causal,
construct, mechanistic, enacted-behavior, or generality claim are governed by a
preregistration. B9 Tier 3 workspace-like functional testing is intentionally
not executable: its present document freezes requirements but now mandates a
separate implementation-specific preregistration and fresh families after Tier
2. Other remaining unpreregistered roadmap items are descriptive or legacy
analyses. They may run only as exploratory diagnostics and may not restore a
failed gate, select a headline result, or consume a sealed confirmation pool.

## Legacy And Optional Items

The following roadmap/issue items do not currently have an independent
confirmatory estimand. This is intentional unless they are promoted later with
fresh families and a new preregistration.

1. **Random-direction baseline reruns (#6):** controls are embedded in Gate 0C,
   Gate 1, B3, EIA, C3, and C4. A standalone random-baseline figure is
   descriptive and cannot replace the assay-specific matched null.
2. **EIA behavioral correlation reruns (#7):** a cross-model correlation over a
   small model set is descriptive. Prospective EIA and D-scale are the governed
   inferential routes.
3. **Generic steering sweeps and visualization (#8/#9):** only the C4 frozen
   intervention family may support an asymmetry or entrenchment claim. Other
   alpha sweeps are diagnostics.
4. **Cross-model steering transfer (#10):** exploratory unless embedded in the
   D-scale common confirmation assay or separately preregistered on fresh common
   families.
5. **Petri/auditor evaluation:** optional exploratory behavioral stress test.
   It cannot substitute for the constrained EIA action assay, and any promoted
   claim requires a separate environment, judge, and stopping-rule preregistration.
6. **Paper figures (#18):** presentation only. Figure generation may not choose
   estimands, conditions, or exclusions after results are known.

## Remaining Design Gaps

No design gap blocks the current execution order. If any of the following are
promoted from exploratory to confirmatory, write a new preregistration before
generation or model scoring:

- B9 Tier 3 workspace-like functional testing (already required by the B9
  implementation-adequacy amendment before any Tier-3 family generation);
- standalone cross-model steering transfer;
- a Petri multi-turn auditor claim;
- a human-evaluation claim beyond manipulation-check adjudication;
- a new model-family claim not covered by the D-scale common assay;
- any alternative layer, direction, SAE width, graph, dose, or statistic chosen
  after a sealed confirmation failure.

## Operational Consequence

Integrity Repair A remains the only executable engineering batch. No GPU, API,
Spark, protected-score, paper, or showcase work resumes until its ten QA gates
pass independent review. After that, follow the preregistration index order;
do not use unchecked roadmap tasks as permission to bypass a failed gate.
