#### E17 pre-registration amendments v2 (2026-07-12, after adversarial review by codex/gpt-5.6-sol; stage 1 running, nothing unblinded)
1. **Scenario-family leakage / circularity (accepted).** M = 5 scenario families × 8 templated variants; pair-random splits leak families across train/test, and the Gemma edit evals reused the same 40 M pairs that derived the direction (norm-matched controls protect direction-specificity, NOT in-sample-ness — this caveat now also attaches to the Gemma E13/E14b numbers). Remedies: (a) B\* selection and all direction AUROCs recomputed with **scenario-grouped splits** (whole families in train or test, never both) from the cached stage-1 activations before anything downstream is interpreted; the running stage-1's pair-random summary is treated as provisional. (b) **A fresh confirmatory M/T set will be generated (new scenario families, ≥5 M families / ~40 pairs, same template + matched-lexicon constraints, E10 audit) before stage 3**; the primary causal outcome is evaluated on this untouched set. The same confirmatory set will be run on Gemma retroactively (E14c) to patch the circularity caveat there.
2. **Winner's-curse in block selection (accepted).** B\* chosen by grouped-split AUROC; the ≥0.85 representation gate is then certified on the confirmatory set, not the selection split. Tie rule made numeric: blocks within 0.01 AUROC of the max are ties, resolved toward relative depth 0.48 (block 15). Blocks 8 and 24 count as mid-depth for selection purposes; selection outside 12–20 will be flagged as a depth mismatch with Gemma (20/42) in interpretation.
3. **Primary outcome corrected (accepted).** Primary causal outcome = **forced-choice helping logit delta (choice_scores) on the confirmatory M set**, with confirmatory T as negative control (prediction: |ΔT| ≪ |ΔM|) and A/F transfer as secondary positive controls. `decision_separation` (projection onto d at B\*) is demoted to a manipulation check — it is algebraically favored to move and cannot carry the replication claim. Code: `--block` now threaded through `weight_orthogonalization.py` and `norm_matched_controls.py` (was hard-coded 20).
4. **Numeric gates (accepted).** Representation gates at B\* (grouped splits): M AUROC ≥ 0.85; transfer M→A and M→F ≥ 0.65; specificity M→{E,T,G,H} ≤ 0.60. Component ranking statistic = `mean_paired_diff` (heads and MLPs compete in ONE ranking, as in Gemma). k deviation rule: if the top-k writer (resp. suppressor) set fails to reach 50% of the total positive (resp. negative) attribution mass at k=6, log a diffuseness deviation and still run k=2/k=4 as primary. DFA exactness tolerances: head-sum abs err < 1e-2 and residual reconstruction rel err < 1e-2 (Gemma/SmolLM observed ~1e-4).
5. **Dose asymmetry (accepted).** Both analyses pre-registered: (a) Gemma-matched k=2 writers / k=4 suppressors (procedural replication, primary); (b) dose-matched k=1..4 curves on both signs + comparison at matched realized Frobenius delta norm; plus individual-component effects (`run_individuals` exists). The bidirectionality verdict cites (b), not (a).
6. **Technical traps (accepted).** Raw-text prompts remain primary (matches Gemma; cross-model comparability); chat-template variant run once as sensitivity. A/B token single-token check already enforced (raises otherwise); option-order counterbalancing already in `choice_scores` (random flips). Decision-tail fallback: a pair whose tail mask is empty is EXCLUDED and counted, not silently pooled over the whole sequence; tail token counts reported. bf16 edits: record realized post-cast delta norm and post-edit residual alignment ‖dᵀW‖ (idempotence check: re-applying the edit must change ~nothing). Inference on paired deltas is **clustered by scenario family** (bootstrap over families, not pairs).
7. **Rubric tightened (accepted).** Full replication = bidirectional confirmatory-set forced-choice effects with norm-matched-random specificity on the same components AND random-component control AND T-selectivity. One-sided effect = partial replication regardless of resemblance to E16b; resemblance discovered afterward is not a success criterion.
