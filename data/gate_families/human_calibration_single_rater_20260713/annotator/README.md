# Blinded Human Calibration

Complete Form A first, then complete Form B. Do not inspect files outside this `annotator` directory. Some items may recur; rate every row independently without comparing it to earlier answers.

Use integer ratings from 1 (clearly absent/low) to 5 (clearly present/high). Mark `active_objective_yes_no` as `yes` or `no`. Use `notes` only when the text is ambiguous or a requested rating cannot be made.

Form A checks four independent prompt manipulations. Rate each requested dimension directly from the text; do not infer one dimension from another.

Form B separates the quoted speaker's current need from the interruption cost to the narrator's active objective. Do not infer one rating from the other.
