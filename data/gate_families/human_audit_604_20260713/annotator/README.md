# Blinded Human Audit

Complete both forms: 444 rows in Form A and 160 rows in Form B (604 total). Do not inspect files outside this `annotator` directory.

Use integer ratings from 1 (clearly absent/low) to 5 (clearly present/high). Mark `active_objective_yes_no` as `yes` or `no`. Use `notes` only for ambiguity or a rating you could not make.

Form A asks about the dimensions named in its columns. Rate the present text only; do not infer hidden experimental conditions.

Form B asks for (1) the speaker's current need for support and (2) the interruption cost imposed by the stated short pause. Rate these as separate dimensions. In particular, do not increase current need merely because interruption cost is larger.
